# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free collaboration environment for everyone.

Contributors must communicate professionally, critique work rather than people, respect privacy and authorization boundaries, and avoid publishing sensitive security information. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing and deliberate misuse of the project are unacceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behaviour threatens people, systems, privacy or the safe operation of the community. Sensitive conduct reports should be sent privately through the repository maintainer contact channel.
